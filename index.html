HDSci Lab

The Health Data Science and Intelligence Lab develops data-driven and AI approaches to improve our understanding of disease and ultimately benefit patients.

About Us

We are an interdisciplinary research group at the University of Oxford working across health data science, artificial intelligence, clinical informatics, and translational medicine, with a particular interest in viral hepatitis and liver disease.
