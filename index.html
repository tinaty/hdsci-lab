HDSci Lab

The Healthcare Data Science and Intelligence (HDSci) Lab at the University of Oxford develops data-driven and artificial intelligence (AI) approaches to better understand disease and ultimately benefit patients, with a particular focus on viral hepatitis, liver disease, and liver cancer.
