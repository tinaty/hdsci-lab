Join the Lab

The HDSci Lab welcomes motivated students and researchers interested in interdisciplinary research at the intersection of health data science, AI, clinical informatics, viral hepatitis, and liver disease.

Opportunities

  • PhD/DPhil students
  • Visiting students and collaborators

What We Look For

  • Strong computational background
  • Interest in AI and data science for healthcare
  • Experience in machine learning and AI
  • Motivation to work in an interdisciplinary environment
  • Strong communication and collaborative skills

How to Apply

For DPhil

We are now accepting DPhil (PhD) applications. Please find more information on the NDM DPhil themes.

Please refer to the University webpages for how to apply and graduate admissions information.

For visiting students

All visiting students are required to apply for Recognised Student status through the University of Oxford. Please find more information on the Recognised Student webpage.

For visiting scholars

Please contact Dr Wang and see the Further Information section below.

Further information

Before application, you might contact Dr Tina Wang with:

  • Curriculum Vitae (CV)
  • Brief description of research interests
  • Transcript (for students)
  • Relevant publications or project portfolio (if available)
