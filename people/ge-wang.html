Ge Wang

DPhil Student

Nuffield Department of Medicine
University of Oxford

About

Ge has a background in clinical dentistry and medical AI research at Shanghai Jiao Tong University School of Medicine, with previous work focusing on multimodal learning and computational pathology. His current research explores AI-driven modelling of longitudinal electronic health records, particularly for understanding disease progression and predicting clinical outcomes in chronic hepatitis B.

Research Interests

Ge's research interests include medical foundation models, multimodal learning, large language models, agentic AI, and longitudinal healthcare data modelling.
