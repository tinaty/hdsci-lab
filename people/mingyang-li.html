Mingyang Li

Visiting Research Associate

Nuffield Department of Medicine

About

Mingyang Li completed her PhD in the School of Computer Science at the University of Manchester and is currently a Visiting Research Associate in the Health Data Science and Intelligence (HDSci) Lab at the University of Oxford.

Research Interests

Mingyang's research interests include natural language processing (NLP), large language models (LLMs), and information extraction from unstructured healthcare data.
